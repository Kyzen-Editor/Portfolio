About me

Editing with passion and precision.

I got into editing the way most people get good at anything now, by taking apart edits I liked frame by frame until I understood why a cut worked, not just that it did.

I specialise in reels and shorts: fast pacing, clean text animation, and sound design that carries the rhythm. Every project starts with the same question: what's the one thing this video needs someone to feel. Every edit choice serves that.

I'm not chasing a studio job. I take on remote and freelance work directly, so the people I edit for get someone who treats their channel like it's mine too.
